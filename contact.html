Dirtygraphik is a Swiss-based design studio created by Samuel Schmidt and Timo Tiffert.

Initiated in 2020, the studio specializes in the editorial field. Promoting the printed object, between art and graphic design, Dirtygraphik explores and diverts various printing techniques. The Ferox Books publishing house was founded in 2023 to expand artistic collaborations and creative capabilities.

Winner of the Fedrigoni Award 2022, "research" category

Selected for the 2024 Author's Book Prize at the Rencontres d'Arles
